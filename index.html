About Kris

Ideas.
Opportunity.
Growth.

Kris Miaco is an entrepreneur with a practical, long-term approach to building opportunities.

His philosophy is straightforward: understand what you own, recognize where value can be created, and move deliberately. Rather than limiting QVCH Enterprises to one industry, Kris uses it as an umbrella for ventures that can grow through thoughtful planning, responsible investment and hands-on development.

Real estate is one part of that story. The broader goal is to create a portfolio of projects that are useful, adaptable and capable of becoming stronger over time.

How Projects Move Forward

02 · Verify

03 · Develop

Improve the project, adapt when needed and create a foundation for the next opportunity.
