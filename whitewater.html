Project Overview

A desert property with room for a thoughtful next chapter.

The Whitewater property is a vacant residential parcel near 16th Ave and Vernon Rd in Riverside County. Its setting combines open desert views, nearby homes, road access and visible utility infrastructure — creating a useful foundation for continued site evaluation and development planning.

QVCH Enterprises is treating the property as a long-term project rather than simply a static land holding. The goal is to document what is known, verify what matters, study practical development options and build a clear record as the property evolves.

This project page serves as the permanent overview, while the media journal preserves actual site photos, markers, road conditions, infrastructure observations and future progress.

Development Direction

Plan from what is actually there.

The next phase is not about forcing a predetermined design onto the property. It is about using verified site information to determine what makes sense.

The working approach is to confirm boundaries and development standards, document utility options, understand access and site conditions, evaluate residential concepts, and then move toward a plan that fits the parcel, budget and long-term objective.

This page documents a project in development. References to possible future use are conceptual and are not representations of approved plans, guaranteed utility service, permitting, construction timelines or final development outcomes. Site-specific requirements should be verified with the appropriate agencies, utility providers and licensed professionals.
